About Navio

Navio is a smart travel-planning platform designed to simplify how people explore new destinations. It helps users create personalized itineraries based on their preferences such as destination, travel duration, budget, and travel style. With a focus on clarity and real-time planning, Navio aims to make trip planning faster, more intuitive, and stress-free by turning user inputs into structured, easy-to-follow travel plans.
